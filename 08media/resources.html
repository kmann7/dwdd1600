Ski Length Starting Point

Use this table as a general starting point, then confirm your final ski size with a qualified ski shop or rental technician.

Skier Type Suggested Length Best For
Beginner 15 to 20 cm below height Easy turning and confidence
Intermediate 10 to 15 cm below height Balanced all-mountain skiing
Advanced 5 to 10 cm below height More stability at speed
Powder / Freeride Near skier height Deep snow and off-piste terrain
Park Skier 5 to 10 cm below height Jumps, rails, and quick turns
