Experience Skiing in Japan

Explore Japan's Winter Mountains

Japan has become one of the world's most popular winter destinations because of its deep snow, mountain scenery, local food, hot spring onsens, and welcoming ski towns.

Watch the video below for a closer look at the terrain and conditions that make skiing in Japan a memorable experience.

More Than a Ski Trip

A ski vacation in Japan includes much more than time on the mountain. After skiing, you can go to a mountain village for Japanese food, local shops, and a relaxing hot-spring bath in an onsen.

Each ski area provides a different experience. Niseko is known for its resort atmosphere, Hakuba Valley offers access to several mountain areas, and Nozawa Onsen combines skiing with the character of a traditional Japanese village.
