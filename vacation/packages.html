Japan Ski Vacation Packages

Choose from three vacation packages designed for different budgets and travel styles. Each sample package is based on Two Adults staying for Seven Nights.

Airfare, meals not listed, travel insurance, fuel, and personal expenses are not included.

Budget Package: Nozawa Onsen

Experience a traditional Japanese mountain village while keeping the total cost manageable. This package is designed for travelers who value skiing, local culture, and simple accommodations over luxury extras.

Destination: Nozawa Onsen, Nagano

Hotel: Nozawa Onsen Lodge

Rental Vehicle: Compact car with Toyota Rent a Car

Package Includes: Seven nights of lodging, six-day lift pass for two, compact rental car, and daily breakfast.

Estimated Cost: $2,750

Midrange Package: Hakuba Valley

Explore the Japanese Alps with comfortable hotel accommodations and access to several ski areas around Hakuba Valley. This package provides more space, convenience, and flexibility without moving into the luxury price range.

Destination: Hakuba Valley, Nagano

Hotel: Hakuba Tokyu Hotel

Rental Vehicle: Small all-wheel-drive SUV with Nippon Rent-A-Car

Package Includes: Seven nights of lodging, six-day lift pass for two, all-wheel-drive rental vehicle, breakfast, and one guided mountain tour.

Estimated Cost: $5,200

Luxury Package: Niseko

Enjoy a high-end stay in Niseko with premium accommodations, a larger winter-ready vehicle, and additional services. This package is designed for travelers who want comfort and convenience throughout their ski vacation.

Destination: Niseko, Hokkaido

Hotel: Park Hyatt Niseko Hanazono

Rental Vehicle: Premium all-wheel-drive SUV with Toyota Rent a Car

Package Includes: Seven nights of premium lodging, six-day lift pass for two, premium all-wheel-drive rental vehicle, daily breakfast, private airport transfer, and one guided powder-skiing day.

Estimated Cost: $12,400

Package prices are planning estimates and may change based on travel dates, availability, exchange rates, and selected services. Contact Budget Vacations for additional information.
