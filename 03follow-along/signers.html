Who Were the Signers?

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence were:

Thomas Jefferson
Thomas Jefferson was the main writer of the Declaration of Independence. His job was to explain why the colonies believed they had the right to separate from Great Britain. The ideas he wrote about, including liberty, equality, and natural rights, became some of the most important principles in American history. He later became the third president of the United States.
John Adams
John Adams was one of the strongest supporters of independence in the Continental Congress. He helped persuade other delegates that separating from Great Britain was necessary. Adams also served on the committee that helped create the Declaration and later became the second president of the United States.
Benjamin Franklin
Benjamin Franklin was one of the most respected leaders in the colonies. He helped review and edit the Declaration before it was approved. Franklin was also important because he later helped convince France to support the American Revolution, which was a major factor in helping the colonies win the war.
John Hancock
John Hancock was the president of the Second Continental Congress when the Declaration was adopted. He is famous for signing his name larger than anyone else, making his signature one of the most recognizable in American history. His bold signature became a symbol of confidence and commitment to independence.

The signers of the Declaration of Independence played an important role in American history. While some names are more famous than others, each signer helped support the colonies' decision to break away from British rule. Their actions helped lay the foundation for the United States and continue to be remembered as a major part of the country's founding story.
